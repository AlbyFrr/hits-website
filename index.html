For authors

Paper submission guidelines

Manuscript Preparation, Submission Requirements & Publication Ethics

Authors intending to submit their research papers to the conference are requested to carefully review and comply with the following guidelines. Adherence to these requirements will facilitate a smooth review process and ensure consistency in the presentation and quality of submitted manuscripts.

  1. 01

    Accepted Submission Formats

    All manuscripts must be submitted in Microsoft Word (.doc/.docx) or PDF format, strictly following the prescribed conference template. Submissions that do not conform to the specified format may be returned to the authors for necessary corrections or rejected during the initial screening process.

  2. 02

    Manuscript Language and Quality

    Papers must be written in clear, precise and grammatically appropriate English. Authors may adopt either American or British English conventions; however, a consistent style of spelling, punctuation and usage must be maintained throughout the manuscript. Manuscripts should be thoroughly proofread before submission to ensure readability and academic clarity.

  3. 03

    Originality of Research

    Submitted manuscripts must present original research, innovative methodologies, significant findings or meaningful contributions to the relevant field of study. Papers containing previously published material without proper acknowledgement, substantial duplication or uncredited content will not be considered for publication.

  4. 04

    Exclusive Submission Policy

    A manuscript submitted to the conference must not be under simultaneous consideration by another conference, journal or publication platform. Submissions that are already published or currently undergoing review elsewhere will not be eligible for consideration.

  5. 05

    Compliance with Conference Template

    Authors must strictly adhere to the official formatting template provided by the conference. Particular attention should be given to:

    • Paper title, author details and institutional affiliations.
    • Abstract and appropriate keywords.
    • Section headings and subsection numbering.
    • Font type, size, spacing and page layout.
    • Figures, tables, equations and illustrations.
    • In-text citations and reference formatting.

    Manuscripts that substantially deviate from the prescribed template may be sent back for revision even before the review process.

  6. 06

    Abstract and Keyword Requirements

    Each submission must include a concise and informative abstract highlighting the research problem, objectives, methodology, significant findings and principal contributions. Relevant keywords should be provided immediately after the abstract to facilitate indexing and identification of the paper's subject area.

  7. 07

    Authorship and Contributor Eligibility

    A maximum of three authors may be listed on a single manuscript. Every individual identified as an author must have made a substantial academic or research contribution to the work. In cases where the number of authors exceeds the prescribed limit, a detailed justification explaining the individual contributions of all authors must accompany the submission.

  8. 08

    Author Information and Affiliation

    The names of all authors, their institutional affiliations and other required professional details must be provided accurately. The sequence of authors should be mutually agreed upon before submission. Any subsequent changes to the authorship order or author details will require appropriate justification and approval.

  9. 09

    Double-Blind Peer Review

    All submitted manuscripts will undergo a rigorous double-blind peer-review process, wherein the identities of the authors and reviewers will remain confidential. Authors must ensure that identifying information is excluded from the review copy wherever required by the conference template. Manuscripts will be evaluated based on originality, technical significance, methodological soundness, relevance, clarity and overall research contribution.

  10. 10

    Plagiarism and Similarity Standards

    All submissions must adhere to accepted standards of academic integrity. Manuscripts will be subjected to plagiarism and similarity checks. Authors are responsible for ensuring that all borrowed ideas, text, data, images and other materials are appropriately acknowledged and cited. Any instance of plagiarism, data manipulation, fabrication or other forms of academic misconduct may result in immediate rejection.

  11. 11

    Figures, Tables and Illustrations

    All figures, charts, diagrams and tables must be relevant to the research and clearly presented. Each visual element should carry an appropriate title or caption and be numbered sequentially. Authors must ensure that images are of sufficient resolution and that all reproduced or adapted materials have the necessary permissions and acknowledgements.

  12. 12

    References and Citation Standards

    All sources referred to in the manuscript must be appropriately cited and included in the reference list. Authors are expected to follow the referencing style prescribed in the conference template consistently. References should be accurate, complete and relevant to the research undertaken. Uncited references and incomplete bibliographic details should be avoided.

  13. 13

    Research Ethics and Data Integrity

    Authors must ensure that their research complies with applicable ethical guidelines and institutional regulations. Any research involving human participants, sensitive information or experimental data must satisfy the relevant ethical requirements. Research findings, experimental observations and supporting data must be presented accurately without fabrication, falsification or misleading interpretation.

  14. 14

    Corresponding Author Responsibilities

    The designated corresponding author will serve as the primary point of communication with the conference organisers throughout the submission, review and publication processes. The corresponding author must:

    • Ensure that all co-authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript.
    • Obtain consent from every listed author for submission and publication.
    • Verify the accuracy of author names, affiliations and contact information.
    • Coordinate responses to reviewers' comments and editorial suggestions.
    • Communicate all necessary revisions and clarifications within the stipulated deadlines.
    • Ensure that all authors are informed of the manuscript's submission and review status.
  15. 15

    Review Outcomes and Manuscript Revisions

    Following evaluation, manuscripts may be accepted, accepted subject to minor or major revisions, or rejected. Authors receiving revision requests must address the reviewers' observations systematically and submit the revised manuscript within the specified timeframe. A point-by-point response explaining the modifications made should accompany the revised submission.

  16. 16

    Final Manuscript Submission

    Authors of accepted papers must submit the final revised version in the prescribed format within the announced deadline. The final manuscript should incorporate all required corrections and comply with the conference's publication specifications. Late submissions or manuscripts that fail to address mandatory reviewer recommendations may not be considered for inclusion in the conference proceedings.

  17. 17

    Copyright and Publication Permission

    Authors must ensure that they possess the necessary rights and permissions for all content included in their manuscripts. Where applicable, the prescribed copyright transfer or publication consent documents must be completed and submitted. Authors remain responsible for resolving copyright-related issues arising from the use of third-party material.

  18. 18

    Conference Registration and Presentation

    At least one author of an accepted paper must complete the required conference registration within the stipulated period. Accepted papers must be presented by an authorised author during the designated technical session. Failure to complete registration or present the paper, as required by the conference policy, may affect its inclusion in the published proceedings.

  19. 19

    Withdrawal and Post-Submission Modifications

    Authors wishing to withdraw a submitted manuscript must communicate their decision formally to the conference organising committee. Requests for changes to the title, authorship, affiliation or manuscript content after submission will be considered only with appropriate justification and approval from the conference authorities.

  20. 20

    Conference Committee's Decision

    The decision of the Technical Programme Committee and the designated reviewers regarding acceptance, revision or rejection of submitted manuscripts will be final. The organising committee reserves the right to decline submissions that fail to meet the conference's academic, ethical or formatting requirements.

  21. Important note to authors

    Submission of a manuscript implies that all listed authors have read, understood and agreed to the conference submission policies. Authors are strongly encouraged to verify their manuscripts against the prescribed checklist before uploading the final document. Compliance with these guidelines is essential for consideration in the conference review and publication process.

Publication ethics & editorial guidelines

The International Conference AUTHOR 2.0 AND BEYOND: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, MACHINE COGNITION, POSTHUMAN LEARNING AND THE FUTURE OF ENGLISH STUDIES, organised by Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science, Chennai, in collaboration with Taylor & Francis, is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic integrity, originality and ethical scholarly publishing.

All authors, editors, reviewers and members of the organising committee are expected to adhere to the following ethical guidelines.

  1. 01

    Publication Policy and Editorial Selection

    Submission or presentation of a paper at the conference does not guarantee its publication in the conference proceedings. All manuscripts will undergo an appropriate peer-review and editorial selection process. Publication will be based on academic quality, originality, relevance, research contribution and compliance with the applicable Taylor & Francis publication requirements. The final selection of papers will be subject to editorial approval and the publisher's requirements.

  2. 02

    Authors' Responsibilities

    Authors must ensure that:

    • Submitted manuscripts are original, unpublished and not under consideration elsewhere.
    • All sources, references, quotations and third-party materials are appropriately acknowledged.
    • Research findings are presented accurately, without fabrication, falsification or plagiarism.
    • All listed authors have made significant scholarly contributions and approved the final manuscript.
    • Necessary ethical approvals and informed consent are obtained for research involving human or animal participants, wherever applicable.
    • Conflicts of interest and sources of research funding are appropriately disclosed.
    • Generative AI tools, where used, are employed responsibly and disclosed in accordance with applicable publisher guidelines. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors remain fully responsible for the submitted content.
    • Any significant errors identified after submission or publication are promptly reported to the editorial committee.
  3. 03

    Editors' and Guest Editors' Responsibilities

    The editorial committee and guest editors are responsible for maintaining the academic quality and integrity of the proceedings. They must ensure fair, unbiased and confidential manuscript evaluation, coordinate the peer-review process, manage revisions and recommend suitable papers for publication. Editorial decisions must be based solely on academic merit, originality, relevance and ethical compliance. Any potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed and appropriately managed.

  4. 04

    Reviewers' Responsibilities

    Reviewers must:

    • Evaluate manuscripts objectively and provide constructive academic feedback.
    • Assess originality, research methodology, relevance and scholarly contribution.
    • Maintain strict confidentiality of submitted manuscripts.
    • Disclose conflicts of interest and decline reviews where impartiality or expertise may be compromised.
    • Report suspected plagiarism, copyright infringement or research misconduct to the editorial committee.
    • Complete reviews within the stipulated timeframe.
  5. 05

    Publisher's Responsibilities

    Taylor & Francis, as the publishing partner, provides the applicable publication framework and editorial requirements. All manuscripts recommended for publication must comply with the publisher's policies concerning publication ethics, copyright, peer review and scholarly publishing standards. Final publication decisions remain subject to the publisher's applicable requirements and editorial approval.

  6. 06

    Copyright and Permissions

    Authors are responsible for ensuring that their manuscripts do not infringe upon third-party copyright or intellectual property rights. Necessary permissions and acknowledgements must be obtained for the use of images, tables, figures and other copyrighted materials. Authors must comply with the publisher's copyright and licensing requirements.

  7. 07

    Ethical Compliance and Misconduct

    Plagiarism, duplicate publication, simultaneous submission, data fabrication, falsification and other forms of academic misconduct are unacceptable. The conference editorial committee reserves the right to reject or withdraw manuscripts found to violate ethical or publication requirements. Appropriate corrective action will be taken in accordance with the applicable editorial and publisher policies.

  8. 08

    Final Publication Disclaimer

    Acceptance or presentation of a paper at the conference does not constitute a commitment or guarantee of publication. Only manuscripts that successfully complete the peer-review and editorial selection process and satisfy the applicable Taylor & Francis publication requirements will be considered for inclusion in the conference proceedings.
